The Anatomy of a BLUPRNT Evening

Engineered for Serendipity, Built for Term Sheets

Every BLUPRNT gathering follows a precision four-act choreography designed to break down formal barriers through movement and thermal recovery before transitioning into high-stakes capital and product conversations.

ACT I // 05:00 PM – 06:15 PM

Courts, Cages & Contrast Plunge

Arrive via front valet and choose your icebreaker: doubles on 8 championship pickleball courts, 85-mph pace in the 12 cricket/baseball biomechanics tunnels, or a cedar sauna & 39°F cold plunge session.

ZONE: GROUND ATHLETIC BAYS & 12K SF SPA
ACT II // 06:15 PM – 07:00 PM

Zero-Proof Botanical Salon

Ascend the grand staircase to the Level-2 Courtside Viewing Balcony for house-distilled saffron-cardamom hydrosols, single-estate Darjeeling chai, and curated introductions by our community concierge.

ZONE: LEVEL-2 COURTSIDE VIEWING LOUNGE
ACT III // 07:00 PM – 07:45 PM

3×5-Minute Live Pitches & Fireside

Three pre-selected Seed-to-Series B founders deliver crisp 5-minute live product demos with real-time GP Q&A, followed by a 20-minute off-the-record fireside chat with a headline venture partner.

ZONE: LEVEL-2 PITCH THEATER & 4K STUDIO
ACT IV // 07:45 PM – 09:30 PM

40-Seat Indian Fusion GP Table

Transition into our glass-enclosed private dining room for a 4-course contemporary Indian fusion tasting menu with between-course seat rotations engineered by sector, stage, and check size.

ZONE: 9,000 SF INDIAN FUSION PRIVATE DINING
