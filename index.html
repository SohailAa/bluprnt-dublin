Common Questions

Everything you want to know before you show up.

Questions regarding the November 22nd dinner at Kinara Fusion Kitchen or The BLUPRNT Round Table? Reach our reservations desk directly at reservations@bluprntseries.com.

Is the November 22nd dinner really all-expense-paid? +

Yes. For accepted founders and business owners (>$5M annual revenue), the entire 3-course Chef's Tasting Dinner and zero-proof craft pairings at Kinara Fusion Kitchen (607 Geary St, San Francisco) on November 22nd at 6:00 PM are 100% complimentary, underwritten by Precision Developers, Precision Realty, and Kinara Hospitality.

Who is at the table? +

Each dinner is strictly capped at 15 to 20 guests. Every seat is held by a vetted founder, business owner generating $5M+ in annual revenue, or accredited real estate investor. We do not admit cold-calling service providers.

What is on the 3-course menu at Kinara Fusion Kitchen? +

Our team is curating a bespoke 3-course tasting menu directly with Shahil at Kinara Fusion Kitchen (100% Halal refined South Asian & American fusion, paired with signature zero-proof mocktails and cardamom chai). Vegetarian and dietary preferences are accommodated upon RSVP confirmation.

Is there any obligation to invest? +

None whatsoever. Our format is built around high-trust relationships first. You will meet the principals behind PrecisionDevelopers.com and PrecisionRealty.ae at the table, learn how other $5M+ owners are allocating capital across the US and Dubai, and decide at your own pace if a future deal fits your portfolio.

Can I book Kinara Fusion Kitchen for my own company's events? +

Absolutely. Round Table members receive priority concierge access for corporate office catering, executive board dinners, and full-venue private buyouts at 607 Geary St. Select “Kinara VIP Corporate Catering” below or visit KinaraFusionKitchen.com.
